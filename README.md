# Gavin Jiang

## About Me

I'm a computer science graduate student at USC who enjoys turning curiosity into projects. I'm especially motivated by problems that mix creativity, problem-solving, and real-world usefulness, whether that means building game-related tools, exploring intelligent systems, or working on software that helps people make better decisions. I like learning by making things, following ideas deep enough to understand how they work, and finding ways to connect technical work with interests I genuinely care about.

## Working On

- `HRL Minecraft Agent`: hierarchical reinforcement learning with reusable macro-actions, structured game-state features, and planning/evaluation workflows
- `AI Yu-Gi-Oh! Judge`: LLM fine-tuning and RAG for card rulings, judge-style reasoning, and retrieval-backed answers
- `DuelMath`: a React/TypeScript app for Yu-Gi-Oh! deck analysis and math utilities

## Exploring

- How AI systems can reason, plan, and make better decisions in complex environments
- Ways to make language models more useful through retrieval, fine-tuning, and better prompting
- Tool-using and agent-style systems that can do more than just generate text
- How to evaluate model behavior so the outputs are not just impressive, but actually reliable
- ML applications that connect research ideas to practical tools and workflows
- Systems that bring together data, devices, and software in a way that feels meaningful in the real world
